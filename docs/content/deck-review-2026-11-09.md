## Patterns across the decks

- **Prepositions:**
  - «ربط مع» → «ربط بـ»;
  - «خطّط» without «لـ»;
  - calques such as «من هناك», «اتجاه كامل», «تغذيات».
- **Live D56 promises carry calques.** Sweep the `services.ts` promises once.
- **SEO wording:** write «الظهور في محركات البحث», never «تحسين الظهور» alone.
- **In-house capability claims** (design, fabricate, build): ask Rand once (the Smith Gulf framing on p.21).
- **Client-specific facts stay with their client.** Don't generalise one client's facts (daily publishing; event apps).
- **Closing lines echo s1 and s2:** change them together.
- **Merge overlapping FAQ fills** before counting against the cap of 8.
