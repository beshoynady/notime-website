## What this is

The website shows **30 services in 5 categories**, six in each. We compared the working list with your **2026 company profile**. The profile offers five things the list did not show clearly:

- catering;
- heritage hospitality;
- private celebrations;
- brand activations;
- fabrication.

We have **not** added services to make room for them. Instead, each one joins the existing service it is closest to, and that service is renamed to say so. The list stays at 30.

- **4 services are renamed** to take in the five additions (marked **Changed** below).
- **26 services are unchanged.** That includes the six you asked us to keep even though the profile does not mention them: product launches, equipment and camera rental, large-format printing, the online print store, e-commerce, and AI consulting.

Each service has a title and a one-line description in English and Arabic. These are the words the website will use.

## Why the four changes read the way they do

- **Product launches & brand activations.** Your profile shows activations in malls and at exhibitions, including the robotics stands. These sit naturally beside launches: both are about a brand's moment in front of people.
- **Galas & private celebrations.** The profile gives private celebrations a whole section: baby receptions, showers, intimate gatherings, and gala and wedding décor. The new title names them directly.
- **Catering & heritage hospitality.** Catering has four pages in the profile, and heritage setups have two (majlis, desert tents, Arabic coffee, heritage carts). One service holds both. Guest welcome and care remain part of the description. In Arabic, «الضيافة» is the word clients in the Gulf use for catering, and «المجالس التراثية» names the heritage side the way it is known locally.
- **Stand fabrication & signage.** The profile describes design, fabrication and installation of stands, activations and branded environments. This keeps it distinct from **Exhibitions & booths**: that service is about taking part in an exhibition (concept, stand, staffing); this one is the build itself: design, fabrication and installation.

## What we need from you

1. **Approve the list as it is, or tell us what to change.** Titles and descriptions can be edited freely.
2. **Do the four new titles describe what you sell?** In particular:
   - Is "heritage hospitality" a term you use with clients?
   - Is fabrication something you offer to other agencies, or only for your own events?
3. **Web addresses.** Once you approve, each service gets its own page, and its address follows the English title (for example `/services/catering-heritage-hospitality`). Changing a title after launch would change the address, so it is best to settle the titles now.
