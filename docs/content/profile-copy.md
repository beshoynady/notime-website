How to read this file:

- **Page** is the PDF page. The printed folio (`2026 · NN`) matches the PDF page up to page 20 and runs one lower from page 21 on (PDF 21 prints `20`). Both are given.
- The wording, punctuation and capitalisation are the profile's. Letter-spaced caps in the layout (`E V E N T S`) are written as normal words (`EVENTS`). Line breaks inside a paragraph are joined.
- **Not extracted** (project rules: no talent or influencer content; no media claims until confirmed): pages 20, 30, 34, 35, 36 and 37. Their headings are listed below so the gaps are explained, but their body text is not copied here.

- **Official line:** "IT'S TIME TO START." appears on every page (OPEN_QUESTIONS #15). The profile has **no Arabic text at all**, so there is no official Arabic line; «حان وقت / الانطلاق.» stays ours until the client supplies one.

- **Positioning sentence** (page 3) is usable as the About body, nearly as written.
