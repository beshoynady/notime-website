# Visual stories: one short film per section (2026-11-21)

Each section tells one thing in pictures, tied to the brand's idea of time («حان وقت البداية» · "Every second is planned"): the moment before, the work, the moment it's ready. Beats are photographs; the existing section heading and lead are the caption (no new copy unless marked ✎, which then goes to `copy-pending.md` for Rand).

How to read: **Beat** = one photograph (and its depth layers, cut from the same photo). **Laptop** = pinned while the beats play (≥ 1024px). **Phone** = no pin, a calm parallax as it passes; it shows the beats as a short sequence or the last one alone. **Still** (reduced motion) = the last beat. Image ids are the registry's (`src/content/media.ts`); ⚑ = a client visible (Rand's approval), **S** = stock (Unsplash), **R** = Rand's profile.

The audience: a Gulf corporate or government buyer, then a family planning a wedding. People in stock frames are seen from behind, as hands, or too far to recognise, dressed for a Gulf corporate setting. No recognisable venue, no logo.
