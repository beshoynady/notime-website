2026-11-17. Method: `docs/review/copy-voice-reference.md` (the voice, the banned words, the rules per kind of text). Inputs: `copy-proposals.md`, `ux-audit.md`, the company profile's own wording (`docs/content/profile-copy.md`), the open facts (`docs/review/fact-check.md`).

**Already decided, not reopened here:** N09 (30 services), N13 (Request a free intro call), N15 (the short privacy line), N16 (the closing blocks), N18 (the Arabic fixes); and the N05/N06 copy in the approved audit-fixes design (the hero eyebrow and its two CTAs; "Go to the services").

## How to read the tables

- **Rule** cites the guide: §2 voice, §3 banned words and shapes, §4 rules per kind of text, §5 Arabic.
- **Facts:** the source a line rests on (`p3` = profile page 3; `FC#` = a row of `fact-check.md`). **⚑ needs Rand** = the line states a fact or a promise that Rand has to confirm before launch. A line marked ⚑ that isn't confirmed goes back to the current text or is hidden (fill or hide, D78).
- **Keep** = the current line already meets the guide; it's listed so the table covers every string.

## Three choices that set the voice (they apply to every page)

1. **"One team" once per page, not everywhere.** It's true (p3: "under one roof") and it's NO TIME's real difference, but today it appears in the hero, About, every quote block, the contact lead and three FAQs. Repeated that often it starts to sound like a slogan (§3, "every section ending on a slogan"). I'd keep it where it explains something (About, the FAQ "Can one team handle…", the category intro), and replace it elsewhere with what the visitor actually gets (who replies, when, with what).
2. **"Request" instead of "brief" for visitors.** "Brief" is agency language; a family planning a wedding doesn't send briefs. The Arabic already says «طلب». EN becomes "request" on the Contact page and in the form; the team's email can keep "Brief".
3. **Arabic: singular «أنت» on every page** (today's choice). The guide allows the plural of respect on corporate pages, but one site that switches between «أخبرنا» and «أخبرونا» from one page to the next reads like two writers. ← **the one open question below.**

## SEO and hreflang notes (for the full table)

- Titles use the root template `%s · NO TIME Creative House`; each page's own part names what it offers and, where people search by place, "Dubai" / «دبي». No page title repeats another.
- Meta descriptions under ~155 characters, one concrete line, no "one team" (choice 1).
- The Arabic titles use what Arabic searchers type («تنظيم الفعاليات»، «عرض سعر»), not a translation of the English title.
- hreflang is unaffected by copy: `localeAlternates` (`src/lib/seo.ts`) builds `en`, `ar` and `x-default → /en` from the route, so it stays reciprocal whatever the words. Checked at apply time with the existing SEO tests.
